A HELPFUL LITTLE ERRAND

He takes out the digital trash.

The idea: drop a file onto him. He runs it to the Trash, celebrates, and offers Undo.

How Trash works in the Mac app ↗
  1. Drop & confirmChoose a file and confirm the action.
  2. Run & celebrateHe carries the icon to the Trash, then strikes his happy pose.
  3. Changed your mind?Undo returns the file to its original location.

Mac app feature only. This website preview never reads, moves, or deletes your files.
